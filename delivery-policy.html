Pickup

  • Ready in about 30 minutes — we'll text you when it's bagged.
  • Park in the "Order Pickup" spots at 1420 Rinehart Rd, Sanford.
  • A valid 21+ ID is required to accept the order.

Local delivery

  • Available within 5 miles of our Sanford store.
  • Minimum order of $35; free for orders over $75.
  • Deliveries typically arrive within 60–90 minutes during store hours.

Florida-wide delivery

  • Available anywhere in the state of Florida.
  • Minimum order of $75; $12 fee, free over $150.
  • Ships from our Sanford store and arrives in 1–3 business days.

ID verification

By Florida law, all alcohol deliveries require a valid, government-issued photo ID from the recipient, who must be 21 or older. Deliveries cannot be left unattended.

Delays & weather

During severe weather or peak periods, delivery windows may shift. We'll notify you by text or email with any changes.

Damaged or missing items

If something arrives damaged or is missing, let us know within 48 hours and we'll replace or refund it. Call (407) 555-0146 or email info@liquor46.com.
