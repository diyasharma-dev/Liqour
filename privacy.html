Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Sample policy text for this demo site.

This is demo content for a redesign project — it is not legal advice and should be reviewed by counsel before going live.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide, such as name, email, and phone number.
  • Order details, delivery addresses, and fulfillment preferences.
  • Account and rewards program activity.
  • Device and usage information collected automatically.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to process orders, arrange pickup or delivery, manage your rewards account, send order updates, and — with your consent — share promotions, tastings, and allocation alerts.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers who help us run the business (such as delivery and payment partners), and only as required to provide our services or comply with the law.

4. Cookies & tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site secure, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

5. Your choices

  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.
  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you.

6. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Call (407) 555-0146 or email info@liquor46.com.
